Third or fourth time rebuilding this site from scratch, but who's counting.

I built this space because social feeds are terrible places to keep track of real progress. Markdown files on my own domain make a lot more sense.

Here's what to expect here:

**Software & Architecture:** Notes on Astro, TypeScript, full-stack patterns, and whatever tech stack I'm currently wrestling with.

**Design & UI/UX:** Motion, typography, interactive HUDs, and making things look intentional.

**Projects & Process:** Unfiltered build logs, post-mortems, and code breakdowns before they hit polished project cards.

No scheduled posting timeline or SEO-optimized filler. Just brief write-ups on things I'm building, learning, or solving in real time.
